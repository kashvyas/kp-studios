Career case study · Comcast Wholesale

Modernizing media workflows while connecting delivery to business value.

High-volume media operations needed cloud-based workflows, stronger production visibility and partner integrations that improved both operational performance and commercial capability.

RoleSenior Product Manager
Period2014–2016
EnvironmentHigh-volume media operations
FocusCloud · APIs · Observability · Services

The operating challenge

Media workflows have little tolerance for poor visibility or fragile integration. Modernization had to improve delivery efficiency while preserving operational reliability and creating new service capabilities that partners could actually use.

What Kash led

  • Led workflow modernization and enterprise platform integration for high-volume media operations.
  • Migrated critical workflows toward AWS-based infrastructure.
  • Implemented observability and incident metrics to strengthen production visibility and shorten detection time.
  • Developed API-driven partner integrations connecting platform capabilities with external services and commercial opportunities.
  • Linked product changes to operating measures rather than treating cloud migration as an infrastructure-only exercise.

Reported outcomes from the role

+20%delivery efficiency
30%improvement in mean time-to-detect
$3M+annual incremental revenue from partner integrations and new services

What this case demonstrates

Platform modernization creates more value when operational visibility and commercial use cases are designed together. Cloud infrastructure can improve efficiency, but observability makes the operation manageable and APIs turn internal capability into something partners can consume.

That combination—workflow, instrumentation and commercial use—is central to how VyasWorks evaluates platform and integration opportunities.

Disclosure. This is a career case study describing work performed at Comcast Wholesale, not a VyasWorks client engagement. Quantified outcomes are drawn from Kash Vyas's supplied career materials.
