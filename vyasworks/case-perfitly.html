Career case study · Perfitly

Building and operating a SaaS platform.

As co-founder and CTO, Kash worked across engineering, cloud operations, product delivery and enterprise implementation—connecting product decisions with the operating system required to put the platform into sustained use.

RoleCo-founder & CTO
Period2016–2024
EnvironmentAWS-based SaaS
ScopeEngineering · Data · Product · Delivery

The operating challenge

Scaling the product required more than adding features. The platform had to support enterprise implementations, growing product volume, consumer applications, cloud reliability and a coordinated global team while the business itself was still developing.

What Kash led

  • Led a global team spanning engineering, data science, product and delivery.
  • Built and scaled a multi-tenant AWS platform while aligning product, operational and enterprise implementation requirements.
  • Established operating discipline across cloud infrastructure, release management, monitoring and client onboarding.
  • Led consumer iOS and Android applications and worked extensively on platform experience direction.
  • Connected platform development with implementation, reliability, security and the practical work required to bring clients into production.

Reported outcomes from the role

25person global team
30,000+SKUs/month at scale
100,000+consumer app downloads
5×revenue growth reported in career materials
30–50%reduction in client return rates reported across implementations

What this case demonstrates

Product strategy and operating discipline are inseparable once a platform reaches real customers. Architecture, release management, onboarding, measurement and implementation all affect whether a product actually produces value.

This experience is directly relevant to VyasWorks opportunities that need to move from concept into a repeatable operating model rather than stop at prototype or strategy.
