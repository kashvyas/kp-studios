Career case studyPublicis Groupe

Increasing platform adoption and execution visibility.

Enterprise platform work across a complex global organization required product roadmaps, governance, controls, adoption and management information to move together.

RoleSenior Product Owner
AreaGlobal IT & AI Platforms
EnvironmentGlobal matrix organization
FocusPlatforms · Governance · Adoption · Reporting

The operating challenge

Shared enterprise platforms need more than a roadmap. They have to work across multiple business groups, security and compliance requirements, shared services, data dependencies and different levels of user adoption. Leadership also needs a reliable view of what is working and where delivery is constrained.

What Kash led

  • Defined roadmap direction for shared enterprise platforms, reporting models and governance capabilities.
  • Worked across enterprise platforms, security, compliance and shared services to embed access controls, auditability and operating standards into core workflows.
  • Used training, demonstrations, enablement material and real-time usage analytics to increase adoption.
  • Established KPI, dashboard and governance frameworks to improve visibility into adoption, execution and operating performance.
  • Coordinated cross-functional work spanning data integration, observability and workflow modernization.

Reported outcomes from the role

+30%enterprise platform adoption
+20%delivery velocity

What this case demonstrates

Enterprise transformation fails when product, governance and adoption are treated as separate workstreams. The stronger model is to design controls into the workflow, instrument usage from the start and give leaders a common operating view of progress and performance.

That principle carries directly into VyasWorks AI and transformation work: adoption and operating evidence are part of the product, not activities that happen after launch.
