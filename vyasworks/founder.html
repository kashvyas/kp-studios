Kash Vyas

Technology and product executive combining founder experience with enterprise platform, cloud, product and implementation leadership.

Builder and enterprise operator

Kash co-founded Perfitly and led work spanning engineering, cloud operations, product delivery and enterprise implementations. His career also includes Publicis Groupe, Amazon Web Services, Comcast and earlier roles in finance, media and technology.

That background informs the VyasWorks operating model: define the problem, understand the economics, organize delivery, make accountability visible and measure the result.

Technical and commercial foundation

Kash holds a BS in Mechanical Engineering from Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute, with a minor in Computer Science and Economics. His work has repeatedly sat at the intersection of technical systems, product decisions, operating constraints and business outcomes.

Regional perspective

Born and raised in Mombasa and later moving to the United States for college, Kash has a long-standing interest in applying global technology experience to practical operating opportunities across multiple markets.
